Emergency Safety Guidelines

Knowing what to do during a disaster can save your life. Read and follow these guidelines carefully.

Flood Safety

Move to higher ground immediately and avoid crossing flooded roads. Keep emergency supplies and fresh water ready.

Fire Safety

Use emergency exits calmly. Stay low to the ground to avoid inhaling toxic smoke and cover your nose with a damp cloth.

Earthquake Safety

Take shelter under sturdy furniture (Drop, Cover, and Hold On). Keep away from glass windows, heavy shelves, and outer walls.
